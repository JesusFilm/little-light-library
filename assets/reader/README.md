# Reading-room backdrop

`reading-room-source.png` is a newly generated background for the reader's
surrounding room. `src/assets/reading-room.webp` is its 70 KB runtime version.
It contains no pixels from the feedback mockups. The mockups guided the
composition: a warm room, soft lamp light, blurred depth, and dark space for
live text. The book, stage, actors, and text are rendered separately.

Generated with Codex ImageGen on 25 September 2026. Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: local static background for a
> children's interactive pop-up Bible book reader. Create ONLY the softly
> defocused interior of a welcoming old reading room, as seen behind the large
> open book in a 3D game. Warm wooden table occupying the lower half,
> indistinct bookshelves and leafy plant at left, soft amber table lamp in the
> upper center-right, deep charcoal-blue shadowed wall at far right. Painterly
> photoreal 3D style with cinematic depth of field, warm amber and dark
> blue-brown palette, rich tactile wood. Landscape 16:9 framing. The right
> third needs dark, low-detail negative space so live white text can be placed
> over it. The center and left can be warmer. No people, no book in foreground,
> no illustration, no text, no controls, no logo, no watermark.

The source PNG was converted to WebP at quality 78 with Pillow. Keep the source
for future edits; the WebP alone is loaded by the reader.
